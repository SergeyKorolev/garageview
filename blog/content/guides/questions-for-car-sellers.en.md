## Ask the Three Questions That Could Rule the Car Out

When contacting a seller about a car listing, it's crucial to ask specific questions that can quickly determine whether the vehicle is worth pursuing. These questions should address any unresolved issues from the listing and help you decide if the car meets your essential criteria. Here's how to approach this:

1. **Confirm the Vehicle's Identity and Availability**:
   - Ask if the car is still available. Listings can sometimes remain online even after the car has been sold.
   - Verify the vehicle's identity by asking for the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) to ensure it matches the listing details.

2. **Clarify Essential Features**:
   - If the listing lacks information on a feature critical to your decision, such as the type of transmission or fuel efficiency, ask directly about it.

3. **Understand the Price Basis**:
   - Inquire whether the listed price includes all taxes and fees or if there are additional costs. This helps avoid surprises later on.

### Sample Message Template

```
Subject: Inquiry About [Car Model] Listing Reference #[Listing ID]

Dear [Seller's Name],

I hope this message finds you well. I am interested in the [Car Model] listed on [Platform Name] with the reference #[Listing ID]. Before proceeding, could you please confirm the following:

1. Is the vehicle still available for purchase?
2. Could you provide the VIN to verify the car's details?
3. Does the listed price include all applicable taxes and fees, or are there additional costs?

Thank you for your assistance.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

## New Stock Initial Message

When inquiring about new stock vehicles, it's important to differentiate between actual vehicles available for sale and those used for illustrative purposes in advertisements. This ensures you have accurate information about what you're considering.

1. **Actual Vehicle vs. Illustrative Ad**:
   - Confirm whether the vehicle in the ad is the exact one available for purchase or if it's a generic example.

2. **Written Specifications**:
   - Request a detailed list of the vehicle's specifications to ensure they match your requirements.

3. **Current Availability**:
   - Ask if the vehicle is currently in stock and ready for immediate purchase or if there is a waiting period.

4. **Itemised Price**:
   - Request a breakdown of the price to understand what is included, such as delivery charges or optional extras.

### Sample Message Template

```
Subject: Inquiry About New [Car Model] Stock

Dear [Seller's Name],

I am interested in the new [Car Model] advertised on [Platform Name]. Could you please clarify the following:

1. Is the vehicle in the ad the actual one available, or is it an illustrative example?
2. Could you provide a detailed list of the vehicle's specifications?
3. Is the vehicle currently in stock for immediate purchase?
4. Could you provide an itemised breakdown of the price?

Thank you for your assistance.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

## New Order Message

When considering placing an order for a new vehicle, it's essential to understand the specifics of the configuration, delivery estimates, included features, and any conditions attached to the purchase.

1. **Configuration and Attached Quote**:
   - Request a detailed quote that includes the specific configuration you desire.

2. **Delivery Estimate and What It Means**:
   - Ask for an estimated delivery date and what factors might affect this timeline.

3. **Included Features**:
   - Confirm which features and options are included in the quoted price.

4. **Conditions**:
   - Inquire about any conditions or terms that apply to the order, such as cancellation policies or deposit requirements.

### Sample Message Template

```
Subject: New Order Inquiry for [Car Model]

Dear [Seller's Name],

I am considering placing an order for a new [Car Model] and would appreciate your help with the following:

1. Could you provide a detailed quote for the specific configuration I am interested in?
2. What is the estimated delivery date, and are there any factors that could affect this timeline?
3. Which features and options are included in the quoted price?
4. Are there any conditions or terms I should be aware of, such as cancellation policies or deposit requirements?

Thank you for your assistance.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

By asking these targeted questions, you can efficiently gather the necessary information to make an informed decision about your potential car purchase.

## Used Car Initial Message

When reaching out to a seller about a used car, it's crucial to gather specific information to make an informed decision. Start by referencing the exact car you're interested in, using details like the make, model, and any unique identifiers such as the registration number or listing ID. This ensures clarity and avoids any confusion about which vehicle you're inquiring about.

Next, ask for the current mileage. This is a key factor in assessing the car's wear and tear and can influence its value. Knowing the mileage helps you compare it against similar models and decide if it aligns with your expectations.

Inquire about the known condition of the car and request evidence of any repairs or services. This includes service history, receipts for recent repairs, or any documentation that can verify the car's maintenance. Such records can provide insights into how well the car has been cared for and highlight any potential issues.

Finally, discuss the practical arrangements for viewing the car and whether the seller will accommodate an independent inspection. Ask where and when it could take place and what checks are permitted. This is a practical request; confirm the arrangement before organising a visit or booking a professional.

## Worked Fictional Reply

Here's an example of a fictional reply you might receive from a seller:

"Available. Camera included. Price 24000 subject to our finance offer. History can be discussed at viewing."

This response provides some information but leaves several questions unanswered:

- **Availability Claim**: The seller confirms the car is available, but this is not an appointment confirmation. You will need to follow up to schedule a specific time for viewing.
- **Camera Function Unknown**: The mention of a camera does not clarify whether it is functional or if it refers to a rear camera or sensors. Further clarification is needed.
- **Unconditional Cash Price Unknown**: The price is stated as 24000, but it's subject to a finance offer. If you're interested in paying cash, you should ask for the unconditional cash price.
- **Records Evidence Missing**: The seller mentions that the history can be discussed at the viewing, but they do not specify what records are available. Requesting this information beforehand can help you decide if the trip is worthwhile.

| Phrase                      | What Established                      | Follow-up Needed                                      |
|-----------------------------|---------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| "Available."                | Seller claims availability            | Reconfirm the exact car and appointment                 |
| "Camera included."          | Seller claims a camera is included    | Clarify the camera function and arrange a demonstration  |
| "Price 24000 subject to our finance offer." | Price linked to finance offer | Ask for unconditional cash price                       |
| "History can be discussed at viewing." | History not detailed in reply | Request specific records before viewing                |

## Follow-up Focus

In your follow-up, aim to clarify any uncertainties from the initial response. If the price is conditional on a finance offer, ask for a written price without conditions if you plan to pay in cash. This helps avoid surprises and ensures you know the exact cost.

For the camera, confirm whether it is a rear camera or sensors and if they are functional. This detail can impact your decision, especially if parking assistance is important to you.

Request specific records that are available before making a special trip to view the car. Knowing what documentation exists can help you assess the car's history and decide if it's worth pursuing further.

Be prepared for the possibility that the seller's reply may not resolve all issues. If key information remains unclear, consider pausing your interest based on your needs rather than making accusations. This approach keeps the communication professional and focused on gathering the necessary details to make an informed decision.

## Ask the Same Five Factual Questions to Finalists

When narrowing down your car choices to a few finalists, it's crucial to ask each seller the same set of factual questions. This ensures a fair comparison and helps you make an informed decision. Here are the five questions you should ask:

1. **Exact Offer**: Request the precise offer details, including the total price and any additional fees. This helps you understand the full financial commitment and compare it accurately with other offers. Record the total cost and any included extras or services.

2. **Availability**: Confirm the car's availability. Ask if the vehicle is ready for immediate purchase or if there are any delays. Note the expected availability date to avoid surprises.

3. **Essentials**: Inquire about the car's essential features and specifications. This includes the model, year, mileage, and any standout features. Ensure these match your requirements and note any discrepancies.

4. **Evidence**: Request evidence of the car's condition and history, such as service records, accident history, and any recent inspections. This information is vital for assessing the car's reliability and value. Record the documents provided and any gaps in the history.

5. **Next Step**: Ask about the next steps in the purchasing process. This includes payment methods, paperwork, and any additional requirements. Understanding the process helps you prepare and avoid delays. Note the seller's instructions and any deadlines.

## Before Viewing: Reconfirm Details

Before you view a car, reconfirm all the essential details to ensure a smooth experience. This includes the car, location, time, person, allowed checks, and document availability.

- **Car Details**: Double-check the car's make, model, and registration number. This ensures you're viewing the correct vehicle.

- **Location and Time**: Confirm the viewing location and time. Ensure you have the correct address and that it fits your schedule.

- **Contact Person**: Verify the name and contact details of the person you'll be meeting. This prevents any confusion upon arrival.

- **Allowed Checks**: Ask what checks you're permitted to perform during the viewing. This might include a test drive or a visual inspection. Record any limitations or permissions.

- **Document Availability**: Ensure all necessary documents, such as the registration certificate and service history, will be available for review. Note any missing documents and plan to follow up.

- **Mask Irrelevant Personal Details**: Protect your privacy by not sharing unnecessary personal information with the seller.

- **Inspection Arrangement**: Confirm any arrangements for a professional inspection, if applicable. This ensures the seller is prepared and agrees to the inspection.

## Commercial Warranty Questions

When discussing warranties, it's important to ask specific questions about the provider, start date, coverage, exclusions, and conditions. Don't assume a longer warranty is better without understanding the details.

- **Provider**: Ask who provides the warranty. This could be the manufacturer, dealer, or a third-party company. Record the provider's name for future reference.

- **Start Date**: Confirm when the warranty begins. This could be the purchase date or another specified time. Note the start date to track coverage duration.

- **Coverage**: Understand what the warranty covers. This includes parts, labor, and any specific components. Record the covered items and any limitations.

- **Exclusions**: Inquire about exclusions or conditions that might void the warranty. This helps you avoid unexpected costs. Note any exclusions and ensure they are acceptable.

- **Conditions**: Ask about any conditions you must meet to maintain the warranty, such as regular servicing. Record these conditions to ensure compliance.

For applicable consumer rights and guarantees, refer to the official [Your Europe guarantees](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/guarantees/index_en.htm) as a starting point.

## Deposit Request

If a seller requests a deposit, pause and ensure you fully understand the offer, identity, and terms before proceeding. This is a critical step to protect your interests.

- **Offer Understanding**: Make sure you comprehend the full offer, including price, terms, and conditions. Record any unclear points and seek clarification.

- **Identity Verification**: Verify the seller's identity to ensure legitimacy. This might include checking their business credentials or personal identification.

- **Terms Check**: Review the terms associated with the deposit, such as refund policies and conditions. Record these terms for future reference.

- **Official Protections**: Check for any official protections or regulations regarding deposits in your region. This helps you understand your rights and options.

Avoid making any payments until you are confident in the transaction's legitimacy and have verified all necessary details.

## Keep an Answer Log

When engaging with car sellers, maintaining a detailed answer log is crucial for tracking the information you gather. This log helps you compare different vehicles and make informed decisions. Here's how to structure your log:

- **Source**: Identify where the information came from, such as the seller's name or the dealership.
- **Date**: Record the date you received the information.
- **Question**: Note down the specific question you asked.
- **Exact Answer**: Write the seller's response verbatim.
- **Evidence**: Indicate any documents or photos provided as proof.
- **Remaining Unknown**: List any aspects that are still unclear or need further clarification.
- **Next Action**: Plan your next steps based on the information received.

### Example Log Entry

- **Source**: John Doe, XYZ Motors
- **Date**: 15th October 2023
- **Question**: What is the car's service history?
- **Exact Answer**: "The car has been serviced annually at our dealership."
- **Evidence**: Service book with dealership stamps
- **Remaining Unknown**: Details of specific services performed
- **Next Action**: Request detailed service records

## After Viewing, Request an Updated Quotation

Once you have viewed the car and discussed potential issues or additional work, request an updated quotation. This should be tied to the exact car and include any agreed items or work. Remember, verbal statements are not verified facts; always get everything in writing.

### Steps to Request an Updated Quotation

1. **Identify the Car**: Clearly specify the car's make, model, year, and VIN.
2. **List Agreed Items**: Include any repairs, additional features, or services discussed.
3. **Specify the Date**: Indicate when the quotation should be valid from and until.
4. **Request Written Confirmation**: Ask for a written document to avoid misunderstandings.

### Example Request

"Dear [Seller's Name],

Following our discussion on [Date], I would like to request an updated quotation for the [Car Make, Model, Year, VIN]. Please include the agreed items such as [List Agreed Items]. Kindly provide this in writing by [Requested Date]."

## Questions About Difficult Seller Replies

An incomplete answer leaves a decision open; it does not establish why the seller has not answered. Use a focused follow-up rather than escalating immediately to a demand for every record.

1. **What if I receive no answer?** Send one concise follow-up naming the exact car and the unresolved question. Record the date. If the answer is essential and remains unavailable, keep the candidate pending or move on; silence is not confirmation of the advertised claim.
2. **Should I request all records immediately?** Start with the documents needed to decide whether a visit is worthwhile. Ask what exists and which relevant details can be shared before travelling. Avoid collecting unnecessary personal data or treating a large document bundle as proof of everything about the car.
3. **Does awkward language mean the seller is unreliable?** No. Language, translation or a rushed reply can create ambiguity. Restate the question in plain words and ask for a specific fact or document. Judge the evidence you receive, not the fluency of the reply.
4. **What if the seller will not accommodate an inspection?** Clarify what checks they will allow and whether another practical arrangement is possible. If an independent assessment is essential to you and cannot be arranged, pause that candidate. The refusal leaves your requirement unresolved; it does not prove a hidden defect.

### Immediate Action

- **Choose One Follow-up**: Ask for the missing fact or document that would most affect your next step.
- **Document Communication**: Keep a record of all requests and responses for future reference.
